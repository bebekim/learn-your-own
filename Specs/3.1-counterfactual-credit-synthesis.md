# Counterfactual credit: research direction and implementation boundary

State: research / partial components (reviewed 2026-09-10)

The July 2026 WCS and COCOA reading motivated better decision records and
per-intervention credit. The retained research is in
[WCS digest](../papers/wouldacouldashoulda-digest.md) and
[COCOA digest](../papers/longtermcredit-digest.md), with the later
[cybernetic learning survey](../papers/2026-09-09-cybernetic-learning-survey.md).

This note no longer claims that those papers prove LYO's estimator is identified,
that a failure-class/pass label is sufficient, or that fractional counts provide
a calibrated promotion gate. Those were proposed transfers, not established results.

## What exists

- [x] Decision, propensity and posterior logging in
      [LessonStore](../src/lyo/storage/lesson-store.ts).
- [x] [Ratio-lift component](../src/lyo/credit/ratio-lift.ts),
      [fidelity diagnostics](../src/lyo/credit/fidelity.ts) and
      [component tests](../tests/lyo-ratio-lift.test.js).
- [x] Explicit registered task comparisons with retained baseline, outcome and
      cost evidence; see [Spec 8](8-learning-evidence-loop.md).

The store still falls back to uniform outcome credit in sparse strata. Logged
propensities are useful audit inputs; they do not remove missing context,
co-delivery, selection bias or verifier error by themselves. A local repair's
removal/restoration result answers a narrower question than whether its advice
will improve another repository.

## Requirements before claiming useful credit

- [ ] Specify the intervention, target task population, outcome and baseline.
- [ ] Retain the actual decision context, candidate set, selection mechanism,
      delivery and known/unknown outcomes and costs.
- [ ] Account for dependent observations and multiple delivered interventions;
      tool calls within one task are not independent task replications.
- [ ] Establish adequate comparison coverage and explain exclusions, confounding,
      uncertainty and scope of any claimed effect.
- [ ] Validate the estimator against controlled later-task evidence before using
      its output for routine promotion.
- [ ] Support corrected evidence and recomputation.

The first two requirements have concrete support in registered trials, but the
end-to-end evidence standard is not yet met. Unit-level counterfactual replay,
learned hindsight representations and credit across edit/move sequences remain
deferred. No new estimator or general causal model is required to complete the
next bounded experiment.
