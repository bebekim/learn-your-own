An absent/empty allowed-tools list remains unconstrained in this descriptive
report. The score does not grant execution permissions. Quarantine is a
file-library rule, distinct from SQLite Wilson status rules.

## Limits

Judge usage makes the recorded judge batch cost inspectable; it does not recover
all historical acquisition costs or provide provider spend enforcement.
Observed recurrence-based helpful/harmful counts are not causal task evidence.
That remaining credit problem is tracked in
[Spec 4.2](4.2-credit-evidence-discipline.md), not closed by this quarantine change.
