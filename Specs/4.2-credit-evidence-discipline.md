# Credit and evidence discipline

State: partial (reviewed 2026-09-11)

## Existing behavior

- [x] [Trace consumer](../src/lyo/judge/trace-consumer.ts) matches a delivered
      file lesson by classification, normalized intervention text and falsifier.
      Recurrence records harmful; absence can record helpful after two earlier
      matching failures in the same spec/writer context.
- [x] Credits retain run/evidence identity and avoid repeated counting;
      [tests](../tests/trace-consumer.test.ts) cover chronology and attribution scope.
- [x] [Run comparison](../src/runner/compare-runs.ts) rejects incompatible
      spec/test/plan/executor evidence. Its single-pair result remains
      `low_confidence`; grouped high-confidence promotion is not implemented here.
- [x] [Controlled report](../src/eval/report.ts) preserves unknown acceptance and
      missing cost and uses a separate promotion gate.
- [x] [Session bridge](../src/lyo/bridge/session-bridge.ts) and scoped findings
      record observations/deliveries without helpful/harmful credit.

- [x] `applyValidationOutcome` excludes session-banner applications, including
      repeated turn/compaction refresh. A prompt receipt cannot acquire legacy credit.
- [x] [Ordinary trial policy](../src/lyo/selection/finding-trial-policy.ts) retains
      randomized assignments and unknown outcomes in a fixed comparison, rechecks
      corrected evidence, and pauses promoted delivery after later failed/unknown
      outcomes. This estimates the declared check, not general task benefit.

## Remaining risk and revised requirement

The legacy file counters are observational. A recurring failure does not by itself
prove harm, and its absence does not prove the lesson helped. Those counters still
affect file-library status and selection. The SQLite store also has a sparse-data
uniform credit fallback. Renaming these facts in a spec does not fix those paths.

- [ ] Prevent legacy observational credit/status from being presented or consumed
      as validated task-level benefit.
- [ ] Connect promotion and rollback to comparable, independently verified later
      outcomes including source, generation, delivery and evaluation cost.
- [ ] Keep insufficient, missing and corrected evidence explicit through policy updates.
- [ ] Validate credit for co-delivered interventions instead of assigning a whole
      session's outcome to every lesson.

The former plan's automatic recurrence/absence rule is withdrawn as a causal
acceptance criterion. Current useful-learning acceptance belongs to
[Spec 8](8-learning-evidence-loop.md); no useful coding intervention has passed it.
