## Revised acceptance

A hook must identify an actual decision where guidance can alter work. It must
record what was delivered, deduplicate applications and credit, and allow later
outcomes to link back to the decision. Deliberate context refresh may repeat
emission; it must not create a new experiment or evidence unit. More frequent
injection is not itself improvement.

The former expected-prevented-loss formula is withdrawn as an implemented or
calibrated gate: posterior counters and failure recurrence do not measure the
causal benefit or cost of an intervention. Current prompt count limits are
operational bounds, not learned optima. Explicit activity tags are user-declared
scope, not semantic inference.

Before expanding cadence, establish a useful intervention with the
[registered comparison](8-learning-evidence-loop.md) and account for its context
cost. The deferred stages must be justified by a missed useful decision, not by
the availability of more telemetry.
