# Lyo learning context

Lyo decides which advice deserves another opportunity to affect work. Its language
separates what happened, what that establishes, and what may be tried next.

## Language

**Observation**:
A retained account of something measured or reported during work, with its source
and circumstances. An observation does not by itself establish its explanation.
_Avoid_: Proof, learning, success without naming what was observed.

**Claim**:
A statement to assess under declared conditions. A deterministic claim predicts
a result for an applicable case; an improvement claim concerns a comparison
across opportunities.
_Avoid_: Treating these two claim kinds as interchangeable.

**Witness**:
Evidence sufficient to establish a particular claim under stated assumptions and
within its scope. The same observation can witness one claim and only suggest another.
_Avoid_: Calling evidence a witness merely because it is positive or direct-looking.

**Proxy**:
An observation used to suggest a different fact without establishing it. Alternative
causes and limitations determine what further evidence is needed.
_Avoid_: Treating a correlated signal as the fact it stands for.

**Finding**:
A versioned, evidence-linked observation with proposed advice and a declared
applicability context. A finding can remain unvalidated or require revision.
_Avoid_: Validated lesson for a newly recorded candidate.

**Intervention**:
The specific action or advice being tried to change a declared outcome.
_Avoid_: Calling the entire session an intervention when several actions occurred.

**Opportunity**:
A particular decision at which an intervention could affect subsequent work.
A prospective opportunity is declared before its relevant action and outcome.
_Avoid_: Equating a session, a delivery and an application opportunity.

**Assignment**:
The recorded choice of advice or no added advice for an opportunity, with the
selection probability when defined. Assignment does not establish delivery or use.

**Delivery**:
Advice prepared or emitted at an identified communication boundary. Its receipt
establishes only that boundary's observed event, not the recipient's attention.
_Avoid_: Received, followed or helpful without separate evidence.

**Applicability**:
Whether the declared conditions hold for the particular opportunity. One misfire
does not establish that an entire repository or task family is unsuitable.

**Adherence**:
Whether the prescribed action was followed at the opportunity. Having received
advice, or reaching the same final state by another route, is insufficient.

**Assessment**:
A conclusion about applicability, adherence and the declared prediction, tied to
its evidence and evaluator. These conclusions remain distinct from comparative benefit.
_Avoid_: Outcome as an unspecified combination of observation and interpretation.

**Prediction support**:
Evidence consistent with the declared prediction for the measured application.
A deterministic counterexample may contradict its claim; one failed improvement
trial does not by itself refute a probabilistic improvement claim.

**Incremental benefit**:
Improvement attributable to an intervention relative to a declared alternative,
within the population and assumptions supported by the comparison.
_Avoid_: Inferring benefit from a passing check, recovery, delivery or lower cost alone.

**Delivery policy**:
A decision about future use within a specified scope, based on current evidence.
Promotion permits that use; a pause calls for review without establishing causal harm.

**Correction**:
An attributed change to the evidence or its assessment that retains the prior
record and prompts reassessment of conclusions depending on it.

**Unknown**:
An unresolved conclusion because the required evidence is absent, incomplete,
invalid or disputed. It is neither a failed outcome nor a successful one.
